1. In Indonesia, what does the abbreviation KPR stand for?
   A. Kredit Perumahan Rakyat
   B. Kredit Pemilikan Rumah
   C. Kredit Properti Residensial
   D. Kredit Pembiayaan Rumah
   Correct answer: B
   Explanation: KPR stands for Kredit Pemilikan Rumah, the mortgage loan Indonesians use to buy a house or apartment. The other options sound similar but are not the official term.
   Source: Pasar Properti Jakarta 2025 | 99.co - https://www.99.co/id/panduan/pasar-properti-jakarta/

2. Which Jakarta area is the city's main central business district, home to the Sudirman and Thamrin office towers?
   A. Kelapa Gading
   B. Bintaro Jaya
   C. Sudirman-Thamrin corridor
   D. Cibubur
   Correct answer: C
   Explanation: The Sudirman-Thamrin corridor is Jakarta's central business district, where most Grade A offices, hotels and the MRT line are concentrated. The other areas are mainly residential suburbs.
   Source: Jakarta Property Market Highlights | Knight Frank Indonesia - https://www.knightfrank.co.id/research/jakarta-property-market

3. What ownership certificate covers a single apartment unit in Indonesia?
   A. SHM (Sertifikat Hak Milik) for the land
   B. SHMSRS (Hak Milik atas Satuan Rumah Susun)
   C. HGB (Hak Guna Bangunan) for the developer only
   D. Girik, the old village land record
   Correct answer: B
   Explanation: An apartment unit is owned under SHMSRS, the strata title for a unit in a multi-storey building. SHM covers land, HGB is a building right, and girik is an outdated record.
   Source: Panduan Properti Jakarta | Rumah123 - https://www.rumah123.com/panduan-properti/

4. When Bank Indonesia cuts its benchmark interest rate, what usually happens to KPR mortgage rates and buyer demand?
   A. KPR rates fall and demand for homes usually rises
   B. KPR rates rise and demand for homes usually falls
   C. KPR rates stay fixed by law and demand does not change
   D. KPR rates fall but banks stop lending for property
   Correct answer: A
   Explanation: A lower benchmark rate makes bank funding cheaper, so KPR rates usually fall and monthly instalments drop, which brings more buyers into the market.
   Source: Residential Property Price Survey | Bank Indonesia - https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/laporan/Pages/Survei-Harga-Properti-Residensial.aspx

5. BPHTB is a tax paid in Indonesia when a buyer does what?
   A. Rents out a property for the first time
   B. Renovates a house above a certain size
   C. Acquires land or building rights, such as buying a house
   D. Lists a property for sale online
   Correct answer: C
   Explanation: BPHTB (Bea Perolehan Hak atas Tanah dan Bangunan) is the duty paid when someone acquires rights over land or a building, including a normal house purchase.
   Source: Panduan Properti Jakarta | Rumah123 - https://www.rumah123.com/panduan-properti/

6. What does transit-oriented development (TOD) mean for a Jakarta property near an MRT or LRT station?
   A. The building is only for transit workers
   B. Homes, offices and shops are built close to the station so residents can walk to transit
   C. The property cannot be sold for ten years
   D. The building must include a bus terminal inside it
   Correct answer: B
   Explanation: TOD clusters housing, offices and shops within walking distance of a transit station, so residents rely less on cars. That walkability is why TOD apartments in Jakarta command a premium.
   Source: Jakarta Property Market Highlights | Knight Frank Indonesia - https://www.knightfrank.co.id/research/jakarta-property-market

7. Why should a buyer always check a Jakarta property's flood history before buying?
   A. Flood history determines the property tax rate
   B. Flood-prone locations suffer damage, higher insurance costs and lower resale value
   C. Flooded areas are illegal to sell in Jakarta
   D. Flood history is only relevant for apartments above the 10th floor
   Correct answer: B
   Explanation: Jakarta has well-known flood-prone pockets. A flood history means repair costs, harder insurance and buyers who discount the price, so it directly affects value.
   Source: Pasar Properti Jakarta 2025 | 99.co - https://www.99.co/id/panduan/pasar-properti-jakarta/

8. What is the main trade-off when choosing a landed house over an apartment in Jakarta?
   A. A landed house has land ownership and more space, but usually sits further from the city centre
   B. A landed house is always cheaper than any apartment
   C. A landed house needs no certificate at all
   D. A landed house can never be rented out
   Correct answer: A
   Explanation: A landed house gives you the land itself and more living space, but affordable landed houses tend to be in outer Jakarta, so the commute is longer than from a central apartment.
   Source: Jakarta Property Market | JLL Indonesia - https://www.jll.co.id/en/trends-and-insights/research/jakarta-property-market

9. When an agent prepares a comparative market analysis (CMA) for a Jakarta seller, what does the agent mainly use?
   A. The seller's original purchase price from ten years ago
   B. Recent sale prices of similar nearby properties
   C. The asking prices of luxury homes in another city
   D. The construction cost of the building alone
   Correct answer: B
   Explanation: A CMA estimates a fair listing price from recent sales of comparable homes nearby, adjusted for size, condition and location. The old purchase price or another city's prices do not set today's market value.
   Source: Panduan Properti Jakarta | Rumah123 - https://www.rumah123.com/panduan-properti/

10. Since April 2022, what is the standard VAT (PPN) rate applied to new property sales in Indonesia?
   A. 5%
   B. 10%
   C. 11%
   D. 15%
   Correct answer: C
   Explanation: Indonesia raised VAT from 10% to 11% in April 2022, and that 11% rate applies to taxable property sales. Buyers should budget for it on top of the headline price.
   Source: Residential Property Price Survey | Bank Indonesia - https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/laporan/Pages/Survei-Harga-Properti-Residensial.aspx
